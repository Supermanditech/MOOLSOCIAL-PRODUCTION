Support and contact

Get help with MoolSocial accounts, privacy, payments, connected services, products or business enquiries.

ContactAccountConnected servicesPrivacyBusiness

Contact MoolSocial

Email hello@moolsocial.com. Include the email address associated with your MoolSocial account and a short description of what you need. Do not send passwords, one-time passwords, payment PINs or private authentication tokens.

Email support

Account help

For sign-in, account access or suspected misuse, use the subject “MoolSocial account help”. If you believe your account is at immediate risk, say so in the first line.

Payments and connected-service help

Use these direct controls or contact support:

  • Manage or disconnect a connected service
  • Request deletion of MoolSocial-stored connected-service data
  • Review or revoke Google account access
  • Ask about a payment, refund or transaction reference

Include the MoolSocial order or transaction reference where available. Never send a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password or one-time password.

Privacy requests

Use the subject “Privacy request” and explain whether you want access, correction, disconnection or deletion. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account before acting. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Business, technology and partnership

Businesses, creators, service providers and technology partners can email hello@moolsocial.com with their organization, location and proposed use case.
