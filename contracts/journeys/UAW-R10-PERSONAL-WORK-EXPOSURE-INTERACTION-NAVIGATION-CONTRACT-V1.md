2. Work shows exactly **Earn Today** and **Workspace**.
3. Tap **Earn Today** to push `/app/work/earn`.
4. Tap **Workspace** to push `/app/work/my-work`.
5. The existing Work owners remain responsible for opportunities, eligibility,

   `/app/mool?from=work` on direct entry.

   `/app/chat/inbox?return=/app/work`.

The shared native surface owns presentation, finite/reduced motion, semantics,
tap targets and responsive behavior. Delivery Work, Onboard and Verify are not
separate actions. This ticket does not promise an opportunity, eligibility,
earnings, workspace approval, settlement, payout or external outcome.
