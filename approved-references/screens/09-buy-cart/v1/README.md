# Screen 09 Buy Cart — founder-approved HTML slice v1

This immutable reference freezes the founder-approved professional unified
Cart on 28 July 2026. It complements, and does not replace, the separately
frozen Retail and Wholesale catalogue authority at
`approved-references/screens/09-buy-catalogue/v1`.

The Cart has one destination and three directly selectable modes:

- Retail-only, with personal delivery, individual products and retailer-made
  Household Basket offers;
- Wholesale-only, with verified-workspace packs, MOQ, supplier grouping,
  landed prices, dispatch, delivery and purchase-order terms; and
- All, with Retail and Wholesale shown as separate order groups and one
  combined total.

Retail and Wholesale quantities remain independently editable. Every product
row exposes its pack, unit price, seller or supplier, route, dated commitment,
quantity and line total. The combined mode never merges consumer and business
terms; its final action creates separate Retail and Wholesale order
identifiers.

The reference images capture the three accepted Cart modes at 390 × 844. The
reference owns Cart hierarchy, density, mode switching, inline quantity and
removal controls, fulfilment separation, checkout choices, total and final
purchase bar.

Product-detail content, Medicine, confirmation presentation, Orders/Tracking,
reorder, native Flutter and deployment are outside this scoped approval.
Native Buy implementation remains blocked until the complete connected Buy
HTML journey is founder-approved and frozen.
