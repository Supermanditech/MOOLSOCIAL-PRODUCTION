# MVP founder action and provider-surface directive

Date: 5 August 2026
State: founder-directed planning authority; implementation not activated

## Founder outcome

MoolSocial keeps a small, highly usable MVP surface. Customer-facing main
actions express a real customer intention. Exact provider types and their
capabilities live behind the matching workspace and Admin controls; they are
not added as duplicate customer main actions.

This directive supersedes the earlier planning disposition only where it
explicitly reclassifies a bounded action or exact profile below. It does not
authorize a runtime/backend write, reference change, build, OPPO install,
external-service action, deployment, promotion, commit or push.

## Unified buyer-surface clarification — 5 August 2026

Manufacturer, Retailer, Distributor and every other eligible buyer use the
same native **Buy** main action. An exact selected workspace may change the
eligible value-chain categories, packs, terms, addresses and approval authority
inside Buy; it does not receive a second catalogue, Cart, checkout, order,
tracking, bill or receipt screen family.

Exact workspaces own their selling and fulfilment responsibilities: offer and
stock, readiness, incoming-order decision, packing, dispatch/handover,
settlement status and recovery. Older Manufacturer procurement and Retailer
wholesale/purchase presentation routes are not rebuilt in native V2. Their
future authorized legacy-route containment resolves to the canonical Buy
owner with server-authoritative buyer-workspace context.

Value-chain categories are versioned catalogue/capability data, not new
screens. Existing beyond-MVP Raw Material Supplier, Packaging Supplier and
other disabled profiles remain unavailable until separately reclassified and
authorized. Complete durable boundary:
`docs/delivery/MVP-UNIFIED-BUY-SURFACE-AND-VALUE-CHAIN-CATEGORY-DIRECTIVE-20260805.md`.
Machine mirror:

exact actors before a child ticket can execute.

### Common seller/provider taps and states

Every exact Buy seller type receives its own workspace wording and permission,
but the common authoritative sequence is:

1. Admin activates the exact identity, category, geography, operating schedule,
   licence and capability after its independent evidence passes.
2. The exact workspace declares **Ready**, **Busy** or **Paused** for new
   demand; Admin policy may define future timing by market, provider type,
   category, locality and declared busy schedule.
3. A complete eligible order attempt arrives in MoolChat at time zero.
4. The actor taps **View order** and sees every exact line/pack, quantity,
   payable ownership, fulfilment method, deadline and remaining decision time.
5. The actor taps **Accept complete order** or **Decline**. An expired, late,
   ambiguous, duplicate or unauthorized response cannot create acceptance.
6. Where separately approved, one consented WhatsApp alert and one bounded
   agentic Accept/Decline call may escalate the same attempt. Neither channel
   owns the order, payment, prescription or acceptance truth.
7. A decline or timeout advances the same customer order and protected payment
   identity to the next nearest eligible full fulfiller within the snapshotted
   attempt and overall ceiling.
8. Valid acceptance atomically commits exact stock/terms and assigns the one
   seller settlement owner.
9. The seller taps **Start packing**, confirms every exact line and quantity,
   then taps **Packing complete** and **Ready for handover**.
10. The seller verifies the authorized delivery owner and unexpired handover
    OTP, then separately confirms physical custody transfer.
11. The delivery owner accepts pickup, records authoritative movement and
    proof, and completes delivery or an accountable logistics recovery.
12. Settlement, refund, replacement, support and audit follow authoritative
    order, payment, custody and proof state; no local screen or timer fabricates
    success.

## Exact Ride provider separation

`Bike Captain`, `Auto Captain`, `Cab / Car Captain` and `Delivery Partner` are
four separate capabilities. A person may hold more than one only after each
capability's vehicle, document, geography, safety and operating evidence is
independently approved. Passenger Ride permission never follows from parcel
delivery permission.

The existing Flutter label `Ride / Delivery Captain` is not production-grade
for this directive. It must eventually be replaced in a separately authorized
reference/runtime ticket; the Admin registry already retains the four exact
types.

## Work placement rule

The universal Work surface offers only **Earn Today** and **Workspace**.

- **Earn Today** shows a truthful preview and opens complete terms before
  Apply for the exact eligible worker profile.
- **Workspace** restores the selected exact workspace and its active work,
  proof, payout, support and settings.
- Delivery opportunity controls live inside a Delivery Partner or applicable
  Captain workspace.
- Onboarding and verification live inside the exact workspace and capability
  that requires them; they are not generic universal destinations.
