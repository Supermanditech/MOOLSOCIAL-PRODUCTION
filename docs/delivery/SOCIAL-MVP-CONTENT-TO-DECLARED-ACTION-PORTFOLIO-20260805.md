## Portfolio outcome

Deliver one trustworthy Social launch journey:

`create or connect content -> declare one truthful MoolSocial action -> review
rights and disclosure -> publish -> eligible viewer opens -> viewer enters the
authoritative Buy or Work journey -> authoritative outcome completes -> viewer
returns to the same content -> attribution and moderation remain auditable`

`mvp_supporting` units. No child is `beyond_mvp`.

## MVP classification

Portfolio classification: `mvp_required`.

The authoritative 45-day go-live plan assigns Days 28–34 to a native
text/image post, YouTube Connect, an attributed product/service action,
campaign duration, moderation, rights declaration and feature flags. Its exit
condition is that content opens and its declared commerce/Work action
completes.

This portfolio implements the complete exit without pretending that every
label in an illustrative prototype is already a real destination. The launch
action must resolve to an accepted, registered and enabled **Buy** or **Work**
route. A generic Book/service destination is blocked until its own complete
journey is accepted.

## Declared-action rule

The action chooser is registry-backed, not a hard-coded promise that every
label works. Admin may configure and activate a display label only against a
registered destination capability whose route, eligibility, availability,
terms, outcome receipt and recovery contract have been accepted.

At launch:

1. **Buy** may open an exact authoritative product/cart decision already
   supported by the protected Buy journey.
2. **Apply / Do this Work** may open an exact governed Work opportunity and its
   full terms.
3. **Order**, **Visit** or **Chat** may be wording variants only when they map
   to an accepted Buy or Work route without changing the promised outcome.
4. **Book** or another service label remains unavailable when no accepted
   production destination exists.

One post carries one action. Admin configuration cannot make an unavailable
product, closed opportunity or ineligible geography appear actionable. The
server rechecks the destination when the post opens and when the user taps.

## Campaign-time policy

Launch UI supports one through seven whole days (24–168 hours). The server
owns a versioned, effective-dated and audited policy. Admin may later change
defaults or allowed ranges through an independently approved configuration
version; an edit affects future campaigns only and cannot rewrite purchased or
active duration.

The clock starts after both campaign approval and successful placement
activation. Approval delay and failed activation consume no purchased time.
Expiry is authoritative and idempotent. Renewal is never automatic. No
campaign pays for YouTube or other external views, likes, comments, shares,
subscriptions or watch time.

## Explicit exclusions

- generic service discovery, reservations, appointments or booking;
- a new provider marketplace or any service route without a complete accepted
  destination journey;
- MoolSocial-hosted long-form video, YouTube upload or private-video ingest;
- YouTube viewer mutations, channel/playlist/asset mutation, Analytics,
  Reporting or Live management;
- Instagram, Facebook or other external publishing in this launch slice;
- paid external engagement or blending provider metrics with MoolSocial
  outcomes;
- creator memberships, subscriptions, broad plans/access expansion or
  unrelated Creator Studio depth;
- automatic AI publication, moderation verdict, eligibility, transaction or
  payout truth;
- hidden phone-activity, contacts, other-app or personal-message targeting;
- live credentials, live campaign funding, money movement, Production
  configuration or provider-scope expansion without its independent approval;
  and
- commit, push, deploy, promote or Production mutation.
