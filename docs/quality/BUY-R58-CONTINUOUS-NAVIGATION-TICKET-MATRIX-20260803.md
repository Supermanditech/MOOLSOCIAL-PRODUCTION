## Founder finding and boundary

The founder proved on the connected OPPO that a catalogue product opens its
detail correctly, but the scroll journey ends after product details, review and
issue reporting. A customer must use Back before discovering another product.
The requested production outcome is a continuous, truthful path from one real
product to another and an equivalent dead-end audit across Shop, categories,
Cart, offers, coupons, Medicine, Wholesale and Orders.

Founder-approved R54/R55 continue to own forward/back transition, restoration,
root exit, product media and detail gestures. R58 does not relabel those fixes:
it adds separately qualified forward-continuation owners under the existing
`BUY-FV2` backlog. One logical family is implemented and qualified at a time;
the qualified families may then be reviewed together.

## Shared R58 directive

Every R58 family must:

- continue only into a real currently owned destination, product, order, Cart,
  offer or recovery surface;
- preserve the exact original query, category, filter, Saved, Cart, order and
  return-depth owner when the customer walks through multiple deeper items;
- rank only deterministic facts already held by the current session/catalogue;
  never imply personalization, popularity, clinical substitution, live stock,
  serviceability, entitlement, campaign targeting or backend recommendation;
- provide a visible, semantic, minimum-44-pixel action with stable geometry at
  320 px/140% text and representative Android/iOS portrait sizes;
- apply `config/buy-premium-motion-policy.json`: reuse approved finite R54/R55
  navigation and R47 media motion, settle immediately under reduced motion,
  preserve one hit/semantics owner and add no perpetual or autonomous motion;
- keep `080`/`098` asynchronous loading/pagination effects and
  `082`/`083`/`140` video/campaign effects dependency-held; and
- receive focused tests, two unchanged-source complete Buy regressions, every
  mandatory protected/release gate, exact source seals and its own unique
  machine-gated/checksum-matched OPPO evidence before the next family starts.
