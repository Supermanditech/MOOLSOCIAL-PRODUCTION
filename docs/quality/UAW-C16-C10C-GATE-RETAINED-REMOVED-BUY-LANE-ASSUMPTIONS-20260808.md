# C16 C10C stale Buy-renderer gate rejection

The first complete C16 positive-gate sweep stopped at the historical C10C
checker. It still required `_BuyDestinationTabs`, selected reveal scheduling,
`readableNavigationCellWidth` and an overflow cue. Those were renderer details
of the rejected predecessor and were intentionally removed by C16C.

The durable C10C contract remains: Shop, Wholesale, Medicine and Orders are
Buy-local, one tap, selected-inert and separate from the one global Mool/Chat
dock. The updated checker proves those meanings, stable public keys, existing
callbacks and one lower-shell call site through `MoolLocalNavigationRail`, and
explicitly rejects the retired lane/cue classes.
