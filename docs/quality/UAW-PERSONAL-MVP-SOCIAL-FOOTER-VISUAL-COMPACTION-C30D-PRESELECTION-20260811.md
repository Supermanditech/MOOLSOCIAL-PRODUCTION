# C30D preselection — Social footer visual compaction

Classification: `mvp_required`.

The OPPO r60.36 baseline shows a heavy separated Social footer. The existing
Social dock, shared Mool/Chat controls, 58 logical-pixel rail and C28F Android
exported-semantics clearance are the reusable owners. No new route, screen,
state service or backend owner is necessary.

C30D removes the Social footer's own top border and elevation, requests a
transparent Android navigation-divider colour, and keeps the media footer on
one flat neutral surface. It retains the accepted left Mool, Home, Shorts,
Create, Feed and right Chat order and all direct taps.

The footer cannot be moved into the OPPO three-button system-navigation region
or lose the C28F clearance: that would recreate the permanently registered
C28D 19-logical-pixel exported-target rejection. Therefore “push down” is
implemented only to the lowest already qualified safe/exported boundary. The
shared 58-pixel rail and at-least-44-pixel native semantics remain mandatory.

Global theme and footer changes are excluded until Shop, Wholesale, Orders,
Chat, Mool, Food, Travel, Care and Work receive their separate founder audit.
Deployment remains held. The fresh APK, when qualified, is only an OPPO founder
review candidate combining completed C30C and C30D source.
