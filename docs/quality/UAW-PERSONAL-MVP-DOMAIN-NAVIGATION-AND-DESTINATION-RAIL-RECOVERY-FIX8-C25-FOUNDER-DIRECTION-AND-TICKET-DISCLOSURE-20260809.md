# C25 founder direction and ticket disclosure

Date: 2026-08-09

## Founder-authorized customer outcome

MoolSocial exposes exactly six main customer domains in one professional, single-tap main menu:

1. Social — Shorts, Videos, Feed, Create
2. Shop — Products, Wholesale, Orders
3. Food — Order Food, Book Table
4. Travel — Bike, Auto, Cab, Bus
5. Care — Doctor, Medicine, Salon
6. Work — Earn Today, Workspace

The MoolSocial menu contains main actions only. After entering a domain, every applicable subaction remains directly available in that destination's compact bottom rail, alongside truthful Back and Forward controls, without requiring a return to MoolSocial. The large destination MoolSocial stripe and the rejected family-plus-subaction popup are removed. The replacement must reserve content space without covering product grids, service results or hit targets.

Medicine keeps the existing protected Buy pharmacy-commerce route, session, catalogue, cart and order owners but is presented under Care. Bus keeps the existing Book route, session, gateway and booking screen but is presented under Travel. This is placement and shared-navigation configuration, not duplication.

## Classification and smallest complete scope

Classification: `mvp_required`.

Reason: founder review rejected the current information architecture and visual shell, while the installed r60.23 OPPO gate separately proved Chat unreachable in an adaptive state. Correcting the shared entry and destination navigation is required for the approved MVP journeys.

Smallest complete implementation:

- one derived six-domain navigation projection over existing route/state owners;
- one shared main-only MoolSocial menu;
- one compact shared destination-local rail;
- configuration at existing Social, Buy, Eat, Ride, Book and Work scaffold call sites;
- focused semantics, adaptive, navigation, protected-surface and reachability gates;
- two unchanged-source host cycles before any successor build authority;
- at most one unique build and one in-place OPPO install after separate machine gates.

## Explicit exclusions

- no new customer screen, named route, backend, service, catalogue, session or persistent state owner;
- no copied screenbook/reference asset or external brand language;
- no change to Social or Buy business content, cart, orders, provider, payment or session logic;
- no new filler action and no duplicate Medicine or Bus implementation;
- no horizontal action scrolling, full-width opaque stripe, oversized brand tile or two-step family expansion;
- no build/install before C25G qualification; no second build/install, uninstall, data clear or downgrade;
- no commit, push, deploy, promotion, Production mutation, credentials, messages, calls or funds.

## Sequence

C25A through C25H execute one at a time. Each child opens only after its dependency, scope, reuse, regression and machine gates pass.
