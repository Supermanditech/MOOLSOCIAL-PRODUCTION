## Audit method

One exact actor and one complete vertical journey are audited at a time. Every visible main action, subaction and nested action must be traced through loading, empty, success, validation, error, offline, retry, cancellation, app switch, call, lock, permission/settings return, process death and authenticated relaunch where applicable.

Every finding follows this closed sequence:

1. Retain the exact observation and evidence.
2. Register any Codex mistake, false pass, false failure or escaped defect before retry.
3. Convert the finding into one exact actor/capability/outcome/recovery ticket.
4. Complete the robustness, reuse, duplicate-search and MVP-scope checkpoint.
5. Disclose the smallest complete implementation, exclusions, dependencies and test plan.
6. Wait for exact founder selection and every protected-boundary approval.
7. Implement source only, then run affected tests and two fresh identical qualification cycles.
8. Gate backend, provider, release, Play and device acceptance separately.

Source fixes may not silently alter an accepted reference, add a route, screen, service or backend owner, expand beyond-MVP scope, or claim device success.
