# C22E reverse-U family bridge preselection — 2026-08-08

## Customer outcome

A thin reverse-U bracket spans the complete revealed capsule family and a restrained stem terminates at the selected main capsule. It must communicate one parent-family unit without recreating a panel, blocking taps or restoring the rejected selected-local diagonal wave.

## Reuse and design selection

The duplicate search found no separate overlay/anchor owner. C22E reuses the C22D `CompositedTransformTarget`/transparent overlay and redesigns the existing public `MoolDestinationFamilyWavePainter` owner as the reverse-U bridge. The local overlay gains only a small non-interactive bridge zone overlapping the global rail top; capsule layout and the global rail geometry/order/position/meaning do not move.

The bracket spans the first and last capsule centers for 2/3/4-action families. It sits beneath the capsules, arches upward at the cluster center, and joins the measured selected-main top center through a short stem. The painter is wrapped in `IgnorePointer` and `ExcludeSemantics`, owns no fill, and fades/collapses with the same finite disclosure controller. Reduced motion snaps immediately.

No screen, route, backend/state owner or subaction is added. C22D and all applicable delivery, scope, protected UI, App brand, copy, interaction and permanent-memory gates passed. Build/install remain closed and installed r60.20 identity remains preserved.
