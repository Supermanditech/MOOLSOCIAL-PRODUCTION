# C33D C33A-C33C shared design and C16E lifecycle binding

C33D legitimately advances `mool_design_system.dart` and C16E under the exact
REG-2312 successor. C33A/C33B currently recognize only the pre-C33C and C33C
design hashes; C33C also unconditionally pins its own C16E hash.

REG-2316 requires lifecycle-safe predecessor gates. Historical machine states
keep their original hashes. Current bytes may advance only through the exact
active C33D assessment or a future preserved qualified C33D assessment. The
complete C33A-C33D chain must pass on both PowerShell hosts with all live
authorities closed except the bounded active runtime source window.
