# Founder OPPO Home, navigation and branding rejection — 2026-08-09

The founder reviewed the currently installed OPPO experience and rejected three connected outcomes:

1. Home is visually unprofessional, cluttered and vertically scrollable.
2. After entering Buy or another destination, users must return to Home before exploring another family or subaction.
3. Repeated MoolSocial/Home/Your Mool/welcome copy, Home icon treatment, decorative cat/arrival content and destination wordmarks consume useful space, while the only persistent launcher is inconsistently named Mool.

The required successor outcome is one non-scroll adaptive Home inspired by the restraint and hierarchy of professional platform launchers without copying Apple trade dress. Six main families fit in one viewport; only the selected family's direct actions appear. A single launcher named `MoolSocial` opens the same connected chooser over any current destination so the user can switch directly without navigating through Home. The launcher becomes the sole persistent brand/navigation focal point and redundant top branding is removed.

This direction does not authorize removal of product/service facts, Social/Buy business content, direct subactions, Back/Chat continuity, accessibility, reduced motion or protected candidate gates. It is registered by REG613, REG614 and REG615.
