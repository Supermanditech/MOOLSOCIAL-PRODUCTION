## Assessment result

The founder's unified-buyer correction removes a real source of duplicated
work. Manufacturer, Retailer, Distributor and other eligible buyers use the
same Buy main action. Their exact workspace changes category eligibility and
commercial authority; it does not receive another buying screen family.

The current native V2 already supports one `BuyV2Screen` with Shop, Wholesale,
Medicine and Orders. The older presentation separately contains one
Manufacturer procurement route and nine Retailer wholesale/purchase routes.
Those ten routes are direct duplicates for future MVP purposes. At least
seventeen additional broad Manufacturer/Retailer ERP, growth, POS, books,
services, customers, campaigns or team routes are outside the smallest
complete founder-defined launch workspace.
