- Light surfaces use a near-white neutral lens with Mool identity navy foreground. Media/dark surfaces use a deep-neutral lens with identity white foreground.

- Buy must preserve product-grid dominance and stable text contrast across the actual Shop, Wholesale, Medicine and Orders outcomes. Social must remain legible over media without becoming an opaque grey row.
