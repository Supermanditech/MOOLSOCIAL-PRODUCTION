keyboard-aware, hides the destination dock while editing and keeps format,
publish and close recovery controls reachable above the keyboard. Existing
session, media picker, draft and publish owners remain authoritative; no fake
backend or YouTube-hosted state was introduced. Shorts remain YouTube-hosted.
