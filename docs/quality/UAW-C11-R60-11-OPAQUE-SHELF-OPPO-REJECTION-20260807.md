matched byte-for-byte after installation. Its fresh Social launch proved the

and the other primary destination content.

The APK, screenshots, XML, source manifest, build provenance and install state
remain preserved. They must never be relabelled as founder-approved. C12 is

requiring one shared translucent 48px family rail and the unchanged global
