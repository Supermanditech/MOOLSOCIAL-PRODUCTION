## Authority

The approved synthesis uses the first reference for clear icon/label hierarchy and the third reference for transparent liquid-glass material. The second saturated purple/pink reference is retained as negative evidence: its palette and broad coloured surfaces must not be copied.

## Approved visual contract

Each existing subaction becomes its own compact 48px clear-glass rounded control inside a transparent 52px destination-local layer. Background remains visible between and behind controls. Light destinations use translucent white glass; Social/media uses translucent dark glass. Every control has a 20px icon, 12px semibold label, strong enabled contrast, pressed feedback and a restrained selected border/icon/indicator/glow. Family colour may not fill the rail, form a broad trapezoid or block destination content. The connection treatment is a maximum 1.5px translucent line with no broad painted stroke.

Counts, meanings and routes remain Social 4, Buy 4, Eat 2, Ride 3, Book 2 and Work 2. No filler feature, new action, extra tap, horizontal panel or global-rail change is authorized.
