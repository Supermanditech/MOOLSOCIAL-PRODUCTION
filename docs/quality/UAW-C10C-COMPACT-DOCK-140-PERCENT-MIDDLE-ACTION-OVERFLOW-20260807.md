# C10C compact dock overflow at 140 percent text

The shared dock's 44-pixel middle action retained 4 pixels of vertical padding on each side. At 140 percent text scale, its icon, gap and label required 37 pixels inside a 36-pixel content box, producing a 1-pixel overflow that cascaded through the Buy fitment suite. Internal padding is reduced without changing the 44-pixel external target, and compact enlarged-text suites remain mandatory.
