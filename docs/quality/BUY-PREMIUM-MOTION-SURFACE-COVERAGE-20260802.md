# Buy premium-motion surface coverage — 2 August 2026

## Founder direction

Every pending and future Buy ticket must assess the complete premium-motion
catalogue across the Buy root, sub-screens, deeper screens, popups, product
tiles/details, categories, Cart, offers, coupons, filters, Saved, Search,
scanner, first-party promotions, transitions, navigation and shared UI/UX.

`Where appropriate` is mandatory. An effect is eligible only when it explains
a real user action, real state change, hierarchy, continuity or owned media
frame. It must remain finite, responsive, semantic, interruptible and honest,
with a zero/static `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` result. No effect may invent
loading, liveness, entitlement, offer, campaign, location, payment, order,
provider or backend state.

## Deduplication result

No new sequential `BUY-FV2` ticket is required. Every current effect has an
existing owner, a prepared owner or a recorded dependency boundary. New release
candidates may be created later under those existing owners, but accepted
candidates and evidence are never overwritten.

The founder-approved R43, R45–R48, R52.1, R53, R54 and R55 owners remain
protected. This catalogue does not reopen them automatically. A proposed
enhancement affecting one of them requires a separately identified successor,
acceptance/risk contract, source identity, complete regressions, exact APK and
fresh founder review.

R51 remains open but deliberately deferred. R56 is the only prepared unblocked
runtime owner.

## Complete effect disposition

### Reuse approved/qualified owners

- Hero animations and shared-element continuity: R55 product-keyed fixed-owner
  handoff.
- Microinteractions, ripple and haptics: R39 and R45–R55, only on real actions.
- Morph, fade, slide, scale/zoom and crossfade: DES-001 plus R40.3/R45–R55.
- Blur and selective glassmorphism: category and R51 owners with contrast and
  raster limits.
- Swipe and pinch-to-zoom: real product gallery only.
- 3D tilt, perspective, depth and elevation: direct-pointer product owners and
  the deferred R51 header owner; never autonomous.
- Subtle gradient motion: ticket-scoped four-colour shared identity contract;
  rich promo lighting remains confined to the first-party aperture.
- Progressive image presentation: fade only a genuinely decoded asset frame.
- Smooth tab and navigation transitions: R40.3, R52.1 and R54.

### Implement next in R56

- Bounded spring and elastic settling for deliberate sheets.
- Bottom-sheet arrival/dismissal, form/floating-label continuity and stable
  focus/keyboard/Back behavior.
- Smart empty-state and genuine success/error/warning transitions.
- Scanner manual fallback, prescription, address/delivery, Saved-clear and
  established recovery families, one logical family at a time.

### Deferred with R51

- Header parallax, sticky/collapsing treatment, pull-to-collapse and deeper
  camera/perspective choreography. R51 remains open for later implementation;
  no successor is authorized by this catalogue alone.

### Dependency-held

- Skeleton, shimmer, lazy/infinite loading and pull-to-refresh remain
  `BUY-FV2-080`/`098` held until a real asynchronous adapter owns loading,
  timestamp, page, stale, error and retry states.
- Lottie requires an approved owned asset and lifecycle/accessibility owner.
- Ken Burns, remote video, autoplay and auto-scrolling promotional media remain
  `082`/`083`/`140` held.
- Notification/live glow, live reactions, typing indicators and voice waveform
  require real provider/input event owners; none may be simulated in Buy.
- AI-powered adaptive motion requires a deterministic, privacy-approved signal
  and explainable fallback; no user profiling or analytics inference is
  authorized.

### No eligible current Buy owner

- FAB expansion, swipeable/dismissible cards, drag and drop, long-press context
  menus, double-tap Like and page curl/flip would create or alias actions not in
  the approved Buy hierarchy.
- Neumorphism is not added alongside the established glass/card language because
  it weakens contrast and consistency.
- Confetti and particle effects are not used for current review/demo outcomes.
  A future real, authoritative order completion may propose one restrained,
  reduced-motion-safe acknowledgement under a separate founder-reviewed owner.
- Marquee text and autonomous carousels remain excluded for reading, focus and
  touch-exploration stability.
