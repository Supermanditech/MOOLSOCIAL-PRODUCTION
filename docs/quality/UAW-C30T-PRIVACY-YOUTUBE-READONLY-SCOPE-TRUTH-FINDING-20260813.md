# C30T privacy YouTube read-only scope-truth finding

Date: 2026-08-13

The public privacy page described Google-authorized “data or actions included in the consent screen”. That was broader than the candidate's exact minimum `youtube.readonly` channel connection and could imply mutation authority that the declared use case excludes.

The bounded correction identifies `youtube.readonly`, limits authorized information to channel identity and read-only channel information, and states that the connection cannot upload, edit, delete or perform viewer, playlist, channel or asset mutations. Static tests lock the exact scope and reject the broader legacy sentence. Hosting remains undeployed.
