## C22 professional target

- One fixed 72 × 48 logical-pixel medicine capsule for every middle main action and every subaction; four actions plus three 8 px gaps fit the supported 320 px compact viewport without resizing.
- One 24 px capsule radius, 18 px icon, 12 px/800 label and 8 px gap.
- Universal neutral transparent smoked glass with real blur and white foreground on all six families.
- No family surface-tone base, page-theme flood, full-width decoration, tint, blur or opacity layer behind/between capsules.
- Selected/pressed state emits the selected family chroma from a clipped internal radial light source; unselected capsules remain neutral.
- A thin reverse-U family bridge joins the selected main capsule to the revealed capsule cluster without becoming a panel or intercepting taps.
- Counts 2/3/4 alter only total centered cluster width; capsule geometry never changes and sparse actions never spread.
- Existing destinations, one-tap outcomes, global rail order/position/meaning, Back/Mool/Chat continuity and ≥44 px hit targets remain unchanged.
