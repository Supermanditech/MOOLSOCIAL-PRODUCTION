# C30T compatibility-suite neighboring-domain overflow

## Observation

After the product entrypoint was made explicitly responsible for production Chat, a compatibility suite confirmed the relevant Book, Buy, Pay, Work, and Retailer Chat handoffs. The command also ran the entire Retailer Business Services file, whose separate compact-accessibility test reported a 16-pixel overflow.

## Classification

The overflow is in a neighboring Retailer Business Services screen and is unrelated to the C30T Social, YouTube, Feed, Create, Chat, or shared global-navigation implementation. It remains deferred to that domain's later design/UI/UX review.

## Permanent prevention

- Select only the exact Chat continuity test when probing neighboring domains.
- Treat unrelated full-file failures as inventory evidence, not permission to expand C30T scope.
- Do not change Retailer Business Services UI under the current successor ticket.
