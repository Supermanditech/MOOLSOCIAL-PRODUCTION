# C11 C10C obsolete Buy header and Help contract

- Regression: `REG-20260807-250-C11-C10C-OBSOLETE-BUY-HEADER-HELP-CONTRACT`
- Ticket: `UAW-PERSONAL-MVP-CONTEXTUAL-SUBACTION-THUMB-SHELF-FIX1-C11`
- Date: 2026-08-07 IST

## Observation

After C10B was corrected, the broader static run reached C10C and rejected the
founder-approved C11 layout. Its source assertions still required Buy's local
destinations in the header, a direct global-rail call and Help as a fifth
primary destination. Those are the exact C11 behaviors being replaced.

## Permanent correction

C10C now requires Buy to provide Shop, Wholesale, Medicine and Orders through
the shared lower shelf; rejects Help as a primary tab; preserves Help through
the existing order-recovery and Assist contexts; and proves that the shared
shelf terminates in the unchanged global rail. The C10C and C11 widget journeys
remain mandatory alongside this static gate.
