# C34L r60.76 end-to-end founder authorization

The founder's continuing end-to-end authority permits C34L preparation and
source qualification. It permits one founder-input AAB, one Google Play
Internal Testing upload/activation and one Play in-place OPPO update only after
the complete REG2729 transaction/evidence batch, two fresh cycles and each
exact subsequent authority gate pass.

No other Play track, ADB install/sideload/uninstall/data clear/downgrade,
secret/private identifier access by Codex, backend/Hosting/provider/Production
deployment, email/SMS, API enablement, quota submission or spending is
authorized. Founder alone handles hidden inputs and account-capable surfaces.
