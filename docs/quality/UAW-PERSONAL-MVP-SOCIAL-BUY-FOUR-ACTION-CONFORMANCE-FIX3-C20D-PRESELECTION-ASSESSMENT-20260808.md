## Customer outcome and necessity

Social and Buy are the two dense four-action consumers. The founder rejected
wrong Buy colours, blocking presentation, weak text visibility and the absence
of a professional per-button system. C20C has repaired the shared renderer;
C20D is the smallest next MVP child that proves its real Social media and Buy
light compositions, long labels, provider attribution and eight selected
states before sparse-family qualification.

## Reuse and duplicate disposition

The bounded inventory finds one shared `MoolLocalNavigationRail` cell owner,
one Social wrapper, one Buy wrapper and eight existing actions/routes. No new
screen, route, backend owner, persistent business state or subaction is needed.
Disposition is `reuse` plus `test_only_acceptance`. A runtime correction is
permitted only if a C20D focused test demonstrates a defect in an existing
owner.

## Gate and machine boundary

C20C is host-qualified. C20D may write reference, runtime source and tests/gates
only. It must pass delivery/scope, permanent memory, approved UI, brand, copy,
interaction, placement, navigation and the C20C predecessor gate. OPPO r60.18
and its checksum remain preserved; build and install stay closed until C20H
after C20G and separate authorization.
