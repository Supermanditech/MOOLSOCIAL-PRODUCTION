# Temporary navigation prototype top-overlay thumb-reach rejection

Date: 2026-08-09

The REG858 revision moved Search, Chat and Filter into the first discovery
surface, eliminating permanent vertical layout cost. It still placed Filter at
the top of the screen. A one-handed customer would have to reach upward to open
categories and then move back down to select the product or service action.

Root cause: the correction optimized space without evaluating the complete
thumb journey or questioning whether category selection was necessary.

Correction contract: family Homes have no category-selection control, category
sheet or filter-before-action path. They present mixed, vertically scrollable
content in which category identity is attached to the relevant card or section.
Customers reach outcomes by scrolling naturally and tapping the product or
service directly. Destination subactions remain in the existing bottom thumb
rail. Category taxonomy may organize content but cannot become another
navigation task.

No Flutter source, accepted screenbook, APK, installed OPPO state or protected
runtime was changed by this temporary-HTML rejection.
