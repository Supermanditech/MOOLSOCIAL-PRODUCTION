# MoolSocial brand integrity contract

Status: founder-directed, mandatory, amended 1 August 2026

This contract is the durable cross-surface authority for MoolSocial identity.
It applies to editable HTML, native Flutter, Android/iOS trial artifacts,
cloud-hosted trial builds and every later app release. The website is recorded
as pending alignment and must satisfy this contract before its next release;
this decision does not authorize a website source change now.

## One identity

- The product wordmark is exactly `MoolSocial`.
- The complete brand palette is navy `#000080` together with the Indian-flag
  tricolour: saffron `#FF9933`, white `#FFFFFF` and green `#138808`. No colour
  outside those four may be drawn into the MoolSocial wordmark, identity line
  or brand motion.
- The identity line order is saffron, white, green.
- The full identity and permanent outcome are the exact `MoolSocial` wordmark.
  A lone `M` or combined `MS` is not a company-identity outcome.
- The approved shared-wordmark motion is finite and sleek: one complete
  `MoolSocial` wordmark arrives with restrained contained depth, its single
  saffron-white-green signature resolves, and the same complete wordmark
  settles. It never splits into a lone `M`, `MS`, `Mool` or `Social` outcome,
  never loops, never animates its layout width, and becomes static immediately
  when reduced motion or accessible navigation is enabled.
- The approved launch lockup is progressive: complete `MoolSocial` first,
  exact tagline `India Ka Socio Commerce App` second, the existing business
  promise third, then one whole-composition settle. The lockup contains exactly
  one identity line and completes within the existing 3,000 ms launch gate;
  it does not delay route readiness or simulate boot progress.
- Do not invent any other initial tile, alternate animal/object mark, one-off
  colour system or module-specific product logo.
- Provider marks may appear only where truthful attribution requires them.
  They never replace or modify MoolSocial identity.

## Mool is navigation, not another logo

`Mool` is the shared service launcher. Its one app-wide navigation glyph is a
two-by-two grid:

- HTML uses four rounded square cells.
- Flutter uses `Icons.grid_view_rounded`.
- The visible label is `Mool`.

The grid is a functional navigation symbol. It must not be presented as the
MoolSocial company logo, and a module must not draw its own Mool glyph.

## Release rule

An artifact fails the brand gate if any customer-visible app surface:

1. changes the exact wordmark;
2. adds any colour outside navy, saffron, white and green to the identity, or
   changes the identity-line order;
3. substitutes the company wordmark, a circle or a module-specific mark for the
   Mool navigation grid;
4. uses an unreviewed module-specific logo;
5. builds or deploys from source that did not pass the brand gate; or
6. loops the company identity, ignores reduced-motion preferences, or creates
   multiple semantic/hit owners for its moving parts; or
7. cannot be traced to the same reviewed source and checksum recorded for the
   trial or promotion.

Shared primitive qualification is not permission for global propagation. Each
customer-surface integration needs its recorded ticket owner, keeps fixed
geometry and business meaning, and must pass that surface's protected gate.
The dedicated shared-motion review entrypoint is evidence-only; production
continues through `lib/main.dart`.
